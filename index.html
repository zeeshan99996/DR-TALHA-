About Us

A clinic that's here whenever you need it.

Talha Clinic & Maternity Home was built on one idea: healthcare shouldn't wait for office hours, and it shouldn't cost more than it needs to. Located at New Sawera Point, near Hashmi Chowk in Makhdoom Rasheed, we bring general medical care and maternity services together in a single, always-open facility.

From routine consultations and vaccinations to laboratory testing, pharmacy needs, ultrasound and X-ray imaging, our team is equipped to handle everyday health needs and urgent emergencies alike — day or night, weekday or holiday.
